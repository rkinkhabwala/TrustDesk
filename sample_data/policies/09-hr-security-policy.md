# Human Resources Security Policy

## Background checks

All employees in the United States undergo a criminal background check before their start date, where permitted by law. Contractors with access to customer data are subject to the same requirement.

## Confidentiality

All employees and contractors sign a confidentiality agreement before receiving access to company systems.

## Security awareness training

All employees complete security awareness training during onboarding and at least annually. Engineers also complete secure coding training annually. Harborline runs phishing simulation exercises quarterly.

## Disciplinary process

Violations of security policy are handled through the documented disciplinary process, up to and including termination.
