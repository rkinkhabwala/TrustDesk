# Incident Response Plan

## Scope and team

This plan covers security incidents affecting Harborline systems or customer data. The Incident Response Team is led by the CISO and includes representatives from engineering, legal and customer support.

## Detection and logging

Security-relevant logs from production systems are centralized in the security information and event management (SIEM) system and retained for 12 months. Alerts are triaged by the on-call security engineer.

## Severity levels

Incidents are classified as SEV1 (confirmed compromise of customer data or full outage), SEV2 (suspected compromise or major degradation) or SEV3 (minor issue). SEV1 incidents require the Incident Response Team to assemble within 1 hour.

## Customer notification

Harborline notifies affected customers without undue delay, and no later than 72 hours after confirming a security breach that affects their data.

## Post-incident review

Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident receives a written post-incident review with root cause and corrective actions within 10 business days.

## Testing

The incident response plan is tested at least annually through a tabletop exercise, and updated based on lessons learned.
