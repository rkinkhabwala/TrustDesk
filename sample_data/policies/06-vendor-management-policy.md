# Vendor and Subprocessor Management Policy

## Vendor risk assessment

Before onboarding, every vendor that will access customer data or production systems undergoes a security risk assessment by the security team. Critical vendors must provide a SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certificate.

## Ongoing review

Critical vendors are reviewed annually, including a review of their latest SOC 2 report or certification.

## Contracts

Vendors that process customer data must sign a data processing agreement that includes confidentiality, security and breach notification obligations.

## Subprocessors

Harborline maintains a public list of subprocessors on its website and notifies customers at least 30 days before adding a new subprocessor that will process customer data.
