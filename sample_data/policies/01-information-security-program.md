# Information Security Program

Harborline Software, Inc. ("Harborline") maintains a documented information security program that protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of customer data. This document is fictional sample content.

## Governance

The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) owns the security program and reports to the Chief Executive Officer. A Security Steering Committee made up of the CISO, CTO, VP of Engineering and General Counsel meets monthly to review risks, incidents and policy exceptions.

## Policy management

All security policies are reviewed and approved by the CISO at least annually and after any significant change to the business or environment. Policies are published on the internal wiki and all employees must acknowledge them at hire and annually thereafter.

## Risk assessment

Harborline performs a formal information security risk assessment at least annually. Identified risks are recorded in a risk register with an owner, a treatment plan and a target date. The Security Steering Committee reviews the risk register quarterly.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to security policy must be requested in writing, approved by the CISO, and expire after a maximum of 90 days unless renewed.
