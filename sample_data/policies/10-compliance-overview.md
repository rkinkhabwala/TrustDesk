# Compliance Overview

## SOC 2

Harborline completes a SOC 2 Type II audit every year covering the Security, Availability and Confidentiality trust services criteria. The most recent report is available to customers under NDA.

## ISO 27001

Harborline is not currently ISO 27001 certified. Certification is planned but not yet scheduled.

## HIPAA

Harborline's platform is not designed to store protected health information (PHI), and Harborline does not sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs).

## PCI DSS

Harborline does not store, process or transmit payment card data. Payments for Harborline subscriptions are processed by a PCI DSS Level 1 compliant payment provider.
