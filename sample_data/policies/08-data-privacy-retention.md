# Data Privacy and Retention Policy

## Data location

Customer data is stored and processed only in AWS data centers in the United States (us-east-1, with disaster recovery in us-west-2). Harborline does not store customer data outside the United States.

## Data minimization

Harborline collects only the customer data needed to provide the service.

## Retention and deletion

Customer data is retained for the duration of the customer's contract. After termination, customer data is deleted from production systems within 30 days, and from backups when they expire after 35 days. Customers can export their data before termination.

## Privacy compliance

Harborline offers a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) that includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for customers subject to GDPR. Harborline does not sell customer data.

## Customer data access by staff

Support staff can access a customer's data only when the customer grants temporary access through the in-app support access feature, and every access is logged.
